# Unperformed human and business follow-up gates

Status: protocol prepared; every gate and result is `open`. This file specifies future work only. It records no participant result, business outcome, adverse-action count or benchmark value.

## Shared gate contract

Each gate permits one bounded public sentence, stated in its section. It permits no broader claim. Before recruiting, operating or reviewing, commit the task, scope, source and candidate revisions, baseline, numeric sample and pass threshold, assignment, roles, scoring, hard failures, stop rule and retention rule. The execution issue links that commit and records the run; it does not redefine the contract. A material change after the first session starts a new run.

Use synthetic or specifically authorized, minimized business material. The designer, operator, scorer and closer are different people, or the record declares every overlap and the resulting limit. Nobody coaches an unaccompanied operator. Fix the denominator before recruiting. Every recruited case, withdrawal, missing record and failed attempt remains in it. A hard failure cannot be averaged away.

Raw evidence remains in its authorized private or customer home under a declared retention rule. Publish only a sanitized summary with actual denominators, failures and missing data. A named human closer reviews the frozen evidence and records whether the gate closes; a name or `human:<id>` string does not authenticate that person or create approval. A change to the participant-visible contract, tested candidate, task, source scope or scoring rule reopens or re-scopes the gate as stated below.

### Shared measures and stop rule

The primary outcome is adverse actions: distinct errors that could cause a wrong or unauthorized business decision, disclosure, identity merge, loss of a required check, or suppression of a material conflict. Agree on examples and severity with an independent scorer before the run. Cite each event to source and output, count it once, and retain blocked or prevented actions separately from completed actions.

Secondary outcomes are useful result, status navigation, total human effort/time, and review time. Define useful result against the task's predeclared acceptance before assignment. Status navigation asks whether an operator who did not do the capture can find current supported status, source, open issues and next action from retained material. Record elapsed time and active effort separately. Include participant, operator, facilitator, reviewer, repair and coordination effort. Do not omit preparation, withdrawal or rework.

Stop further candidate exposure if candidate adverse actions exceed the matched baseline. Preserve the failing evidence and route it to the responsible reviewer. Do not offset the excess with time saved, answer completeness or participant preference. Equal counts do not prove non-inferiority. Any unsupported owner, approval or rule authority, or action outside the authorized task boundary, is a hard failure.

Every comparative gate needs a same-scope manual-baseline record or case, prepared independently with the declared current procedure. Do not show the same case or message to one operator in both arms. Score candidate and baseline together under masked labels. If a defensible pair cannot be prepared, leave comparative outcomes and the stop-rule comparison `open`.

## Gate A — unaccompanied first use and correction

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “`<k>` of `<N>` eligible first-time participants completed the frozen no-install task and one source correction without help and without a hard failure, at candidate revision `<revision>`.” This licenses no claim about other people, tasks, ease, time saving or population rates.

The numeric values of `<N>` and `<k>` are `open`. Commit them before recruiting; until then this gate cannot run.

Recruit people who do business work in a small or medium company and have no earlier exposure to IMPACTS files, demonstrations or courses. Record only broad role categories. Exclude anyone who designed the tasks and anyone whose main job is software development. Run each participant separately with the same frozen instructions and fresh bounded material. No facilitator gives hints. A silent observer may record time and steps and may intervene only to stop unsafe action; the intervention makes that task assisted and therefore failed for this gate.

Each participant completes two tasks:

1. the frozen first-win task;
2. one frozen source correction delivered after the first draft, requiring the participant to update the current result, retain the earlier source trail and leave unsupported values `open`.

Prepare a same-scope manual-baseline case through an independent operator. No operator sees the same case in both arms. A scorer who neither designed nor observed the tasks grades frozen files, not self-report. Score both tasks, source traceability, `open` gaps, status and next action, adverse actions, total effort/time and review time.

Every recruited participant remains in the denominator; withdrawal or an incomplete record is a failure with its reason. Closure requires the frozen numeric threshold, both tasks unaided and no hard failure in any session. If a participant exposes a defect, retain the exact pre-repair state and blocker. Repair its single authoritative home only after the session and test the changed revision with a new eligible participant. Keep pre- and post-repair results separate.

Private evidence retains consent, session records, files, timing and scoring under the declared retention rule. The public record contains only sanitized counts, broad role categories, denominators, hard failures and blockers. The named closer records the decision. Reopen when the participant-visible first-win or correction instructions change.

## Gate B — second-operator continuation after 168 elapsed hours

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “After at least 168 hours, a second operator continued the frozen case from its retained files alone, found the changed source and open decision, and took no unauthorized action; the matched manual case is reported beside it, at candidate revision `<revision>`.” This licenses no claim about other people, cases or time saving.

Before the first operator starts, freeze one authorized or synthetic bounded case, its required result, one source change that will occur during the gap, at least one human decision still open at handoff, and the list of open questions used for scoring. Freeze a same-scope manual case for a different second operator.

At least 168 full elapsed hours after the first operator freezes the candidate case, give a different operator only its retained router and authorized files. Give no verbal briefing, chat history or private memory. Ask the operator to state supported status, find the changed source, recover the frozen open questions and name the next permitted action. Run the same protocol on the manual case. A blind scorer grades both.

Use recorded commit or file times to establish elapsed time. Any contact with the first operator during continuation breaks that arm: retain the record and restart with a new case. Missing links, sources or revisions remain scored gaps; the second operator does not fill them from the first operator. Closure requires successful candidate continuation without a hard failure and publication of both arms' sanitized outcomes, active effort and elapsed time. The named closer records the decision. Reopen when the router, case instructions or participant-visible templates change.

## Gate C — bounded domain review

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “A `<reviewer category>` reviewed the bounded flow `<flow>` at revision `<revision>` and reported `<sanitized findings, including negative findings>`.” This licenses no compliance, certification, ownership, approval-authority or other-flow claim.

The flow, reviewer category and review questions are `open`. Commit them before review; until then this gate cannot run. Define the reviewer's demonstrated domain and decision scope, the claims inside it and those outside it. Record pay and relationship to the maintainer. The reviewer did not design the flow and receives no coaching.

Freeze the questions before providing evidence. They cover at least traceability of each material figure to source and rule, missing required checks, separation of preparation from decision authority, relevant retention conflict and the effect of coordinated content-and-digest rewriting within this flow. Provide masked candidate and manual material when making a comparison.

Score supported findings, missed material conditions, unsupported claims, unresolved disagreements, adverse actions, review time and total effort. Preserve comments and corrections as separate evidence. Out-of-scope findings remain `open` routing inputs. Closure requires a retained scoped review, a disposition for every material gap and public wording limited to the licensed sentence. The named closer records the decision. Reopen when the flow, material sources, review questions or applicable retention rule changes.

## Gate D — matched business comparison

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “Across `<N>` matched pairs of `<task>` at `<business scope>`, IMPACTS produced `<x>` adverse actions against `<y>` for the manual baseline, with `<useful-result and total-effort measures>`, at candidate revision `<revision>`.” This licenses no ROI, speed or effect claim for other businesses, tasks or revisions.

The numeric value of `<N>` is `open`. Commit it before case selection; until then this gate cannot run. Use cases the investigators have not seen in earlier trials and disclose any prior exposure. Before assignment, commit the result, recipient, acceptance definition, case frame, period, unit, source revisions, required checks, arm procedures, counterbalancing, severity scale and stop rule. Pair cases within the same declared scope. Do not give one operator the same case in both arms.

A scorer who did not design, operate, observe or coach either arm grades masked results. Without that scorer the run may be reported but cannot close this gate. Score candidate adverse actions first, then useful whole results, status navigation, total human effort/time and review time. Every selected pair, missing input, withdrawal and failed attempt stays in the denominator. Report unmatched cases separately.

If candidate adverse actions exceed baseline, stop benefit and campaign claims and repair the boundary before another frozen run. No hard failure is permitted. Closure requires the frozen pass rule, a public sanitized aggregate and the named closer's recorded decision. Reopen when the candidate revision, task, business scope, case frame or scoring rule changes.

## Result record and completion

For each gate, retain the frozen contract commit, owner, operator, scorer and closer; candidate and baseline revisions; assignment; source IDs; anonymized outputs; per-case outcome; adverse-action evidence; all effort and timing; withdrawals; exclusions; disagreements; repairs and remaining gaps. Do not include unnecessary personal data. Report adverse actions before secondary measures.

All gates remain `open` until their stated evidence exists and the named closer has checked the bounded claim. A result from one gate does not satisfy another. No unperformed score, pass, benefit or reduction may be inferred from this protocol.
