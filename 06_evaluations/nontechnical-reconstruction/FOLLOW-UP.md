# Unperformed human and business follow-up gates

Status: protocol prepared; every gate and result is `open`. This file specifies future work only. It records no participant result, business outcome, adverse-action count or benchmark value.

## Shared gate contract

Before recruiting, operating or reviewing, commit the task, scope, source and candidate revisions, baseline, numeric sample and pass threshold, assignment, roles, scoring, hard failures, stop rule and retention rule. A gate cannot run while any of these is `open`. A material change after the first session starts a new run. Preserve raw evidence and prior answers; never rewrite a failed answer after the scorer sees it.

Use synthetic or specifically authorized, minimized business material. The designer, operator, scorer and closer are different people, or the record declares every overlap and its limit. Nobody coaches an operator. Every recruited case, withdrawal, missing record and failed attempt stays in the denominator. A hard failure cannot be averaged away.

Evidence handling, public summaries and closure follow the [contribution guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#external-adoption-and-deployment-gates).

### Shared measures and stop rule

The primary outcome is adverse actions: distinct errors that could cause a wrong or unauthorized business decision, disclosure, identity merge, loss of a required check, or suppression of a material conflict. Agree on examples and severity with an independent scorer before the run. Cite each event to source and output, count it once, and retain blocked or prevented actions separately from completed actions.

Secondary outcomes are useful result, status navigation, total human effort/time, and review time. Define useful result against the task's predeclared acceptance before assignment. Status navigation asks whether an operator who did not do the capture can find current supported status, source, open issues and next action from retained material. Record elapsed time and active effort separately. Include participant, operator, facilitator, reviewer, repair and coordination effort. Do not omit preparation, withdrawal or rework.

Stop further candidate exposure if candidate adverse actions exceed the matched baseline, and stop any benefit or campaign claim. Preserve the failing evidence and route it to the responsible reviewer. Do not offset the excess with time saved, answer completeness or participant preference. Equal counts do not prove non-inferiority. Any unsupported owner, approval or rule authority, or action outside the authorized task boundary, is a hard failure.

Every comparative gate needs a same-scope manual-baseline record or case, prepared independently with the declared current procedure. Do not show the same case or message to one operator in both arms. Score candidate and baseline together under masked labels. If a defensible pair cannot be prepared, leave comparative outcomes and the stop-rule comparison `open`.

## Gate A — unaccompanied first use and correction

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “`<k>` of `<N>` eligible first-time participants completed the frozen no-install task and one source correction without help and without a hard failure, at candidate revision `<revision>`.”

Recruit people who do business work in a small or medium company and have no earlier exposure to IMPACTS files, demonstrations or courses. Record only broad role categories. Exclude anyone whose main job is software development. Run each participant separately with the same frozen instructions and fresh bounded material. Use the participants' working language for every instruction and exercise file. A silent observer may record time and steps and may intervene only to stop unsafe action; the intervention makes that task assisted and therefore failed for this gate.

Each participant completes two tasks:

1. the frozen first-win task;
2. one frozen source correction delivered after the first draft, requiring the participant to update the current result, retain the earlier source trail and leave unsupported values `open`.

A scorer who did not observe grades the frozen files, not self-report, on both tasks, source traceability and preserved `open` gaps, plus the shared measures.

Closure requires at least `<k>` of `<N>` participants completing both tasks unaided, and no hard failure in any session. If a participant exposes a defect, retain the exact pre-repair state and blocker. Repair its single authoritative home only after the session and test the changed revision with a new eligible participant. Keep pre- and post-repair results separate.

## Gate B — second-operator continuation after 168 elapsed hours

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “After at least 168 hours, a second operator continued the frozen case from its retained files alone, found the changed source and open decision, and took no unauthorized action, at candidate revision `<revision>`.”

Before the first operator starts, freeze one authorized or synthetic bounded case, its required result, one source change that will occur during the gap, at least one human decision still open at handoff, and the list of open questions used for scoring.

At least 168 full elapsed hours after the first operator freezes the candidate case, give a different operator only its retained router and authorized files. Give no verbal briefing, chat history or private memory. Ask the operator to state supported status, find the changed source, recover the frozen open questions and name the next permitted action.

Use recorded commit or file times to establish elapsed time. Any contact with the first operator during continuation breaks the run: retain the record and restart with a new case. Missing links, sources or revisions remain scored gaps. Closure requires successful candidate continuation without a hard failure.

## Gate C — bounded domain review

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “A `<reviewer category>` reviewed the bounded flow `<flow>` at revision `<revision>` and reported `<sanitized findings, including negative findings>`.” A review does not establish compliance, source ownership or approval authority.

Define the reviewer's demonstrated domain and decision scope, the claims inside it and those outside it. Record pay and relationship to the maintainer.

The committed questions cover at least traceability of each material figure to source and rule, missing required checks, separation of preparation from decision authority, relevant retention conflict and the effect of coordinated content-and-digest rewriting within this flow.

Score supported findings, missed material conditions, unsupported claims and unresolved disagreements, with the shared measures. Preserve comments and corrections as separate evidence. Out-of-scope findings remain `open` routing inputs. Closure requires a retained scoped review and a disposition for every material gap.

## Gate D — matched business comparison

**Licensed sentence after closure:** “Across `<N>` matched pairs of `<task>` at `<business scope>`, IMPACTS produced `<x>` adverse actions against `<y>` for the manual baseline, with `<useful-result and total-effort measures>`, at candidate revision `<revision>`.”

Use cases the investigators have not seen in earlier trials and disclose any prior exposure. The committed design also names the recipient, acceptance definition, case frame, period, unit, required checks and counterbalancing. Pair cases within the same declared scope; do not merge populations or time periods that do not match.

Without a scorer who did not design, operate, observe or coach either arm, the run may be reported but cannot close this gate. Report unmatched cases separately.

Closure requires the frozen pass rule.

## Result record and completion

For each gate, retain the frozen contract commit, owner, operator, scorer and closer; consent; candidate and baseline revisions; assignment; source IDs; anonymized outputs; per-case outcome; adverse-action evidence; all effort and timing; withdrawals; exclusions; disagreements; repairs and remaining gaps. Do not include unnecessary personal data. Report adverse actions before secondary measures.

All gates remain `open` until their stated evidence exists and the named closer has checked the bounded claim. A result from one gate does not satisfy another. No unperformed score, pass, benefit or reduction may be inferred from this protocol.
