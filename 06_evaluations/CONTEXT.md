# Evaluations

This directory contains optional deterministic Core checks and synthetic reading cases. Load only the branch needed by the question; synthetic fixtures establish no customer facts, authority or business effects.

- [Cold walk](cold-walk/CONTEXT.md): execution state, loop/wait/gate and handoff.
- [Computation walk](computation-walk/CONTEXT.md): independent synthetic calculation and dependent blockers.
- [Offer walk](offer-walk/CONTEXT.md): fixed German/English output, controlled source handover and reviewed-correction fixtures; commercial claims route to the ontology/example.
- [Delivery replay](delivery-replay/CONTEXT.md): deterministic synthetic delivery calculation with a resumable workspace and an open human decision.
- [Nontechnical reconstruction](nontechnical-reconstruction/CONTEXT.md): shared reading instructions, separate spreadsheet-reconstruction, work-design and listening cases, blinded expectations and external follow-up gates.
- [Complexity budget](complexity-budget/CONTEXT.md): declared structural metrics.
- [Structural conformance corpus](conformance/CONTEXT.md): frozen input/output cases against a supplied CLI command, independent-implementation challenge and declared public-scope inventory; passing covers only these cases.

Package evaluations live with their package. Customer regression material stays in the customer repository. Do not load sibling walks or exhaust their outgoing links.
