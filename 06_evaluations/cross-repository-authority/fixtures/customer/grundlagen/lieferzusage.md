# Delivery promise

## Definition

At `customer@c8`, this file owns the local meaning of a delivery promise: the date communicated to the customer as a commitment. Case inputs are `reported`; availability for the case is `open`. The [method estimator](../../method/skills/delivery-estimate/SKILL.md) supplies a date estimate. That link locates a reusable calculation; it does not transfer this definition's authority or establish a commitment.
