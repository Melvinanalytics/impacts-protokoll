# Offer terms

## Delivery

At `sales@s3`, this rule owns the conditions for a delivery commitment in an offer. It needs an [estimate](../../method/skills/delivery-estimate/SKILL.md), matching validated [availability evidence](../../method/skills/availability-check/SKILL.md) and the applicable human decision. The [customer's delivery-promise definition](../../customer/grundlagen/lieferzusage.md#definition) supplies local meaning. No availability evidence or human decision is established in this fixture. The accepted result is therefore a stated blocker, not a delivery commitment.
