## What may be simplified from these premises?

There is no sound unconditional row-by-row reduction because rule provenance, actual repeated executions, failure causes, source equivalence and exception coverage are incomplete. Ask the earlier decisive question for each activity rather than requiring one universal questionnaire before any permitted work.
