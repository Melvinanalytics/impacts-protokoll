## Supplied procedure

The document is titled “Process a Vendor Invoice.” Its purpose is accurate, timely entry of vendor invoices. Office Manager is its document owner and escalation contact. The primary performer is Office Coordinator; Accounting answers questions; Owner/Finance approves when required. “Owner” in individual instructions is not disambiguated by an authority record.

Actual source revisions, configured operations, permitted effects, individual rights holders, legal jurisdiction and rule provenance are not supplied.

Rules say to match a PO if available, use exact vendor details, choose the correct category, include invoice number/date/amount/due date, avoid scheduling payment above $1,000 without approval, and save the original in Drive.
