## Completion rubric

A reading passes only when it:

- answers both questions independently and retains `open` where a premise is missing;
- treats a required check, evidence item or authorized decision as possible result work without transferring its authority;
- minimizes unnecessary coordination before assigning an executor;
- selects deterministic, agent and human contributions from rules, variance, interaction, authority, effects and available operations rather than the classification words;
- keeps waiting separate from activity and does not split a coherent workstep solely to create exclusive labels;
- aggregates non-overlapping observed effort once by the four known combinations, with mixed/open effort and waiting separate;
- treats “neither” and an avoidability claim as a redesign hypothesis until the counter-design preserves required results, checks, decisions, authority and successor inputs.

For every case, the reader must also identify blocked use, unresolved premises and the governing source path. Where a physical or external effect remains, the answer must state its permission, current-state check and completion evidence or mark each missing item `open`. The table above supplies the expected distinctions; the method and automation boundary linked in the opening paragraph remain the authorities.
