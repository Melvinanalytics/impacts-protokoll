# Worked expectations

This is the answer key for the [synthetic Augment walk](CONTEXT.md), not a second protocol authority or execution evidence. The method's [Augment](../../02_protocol/impacts-method.md#augment), the Architect's [automation boundary](../../02_protocol/impacts-architect/references/zuschnitt.md#automation-boundary), the [workstep template](../../02_protocol/templates/arbeitsschritt.md) and the declared synthetic premises govern the answers.

| Case | Expected context design and boundary |
|---|---|
| Current treasury rate | Reuse and route the portal value by currency pair and offer date into the calculation input before execution. Retain portal identity, approved revision/as-of time, actual read time and required source check. Replace the email dependency only after the controlled read proves equivalent. The rate supplies neither calculation correctness nor commitment authority. |
| Tacit exception | Acquire a dated reported claim from the worker with scope and concrete examples. Keep rule validity and ownership `open`; do not promote the report to the maintained rule or make the worker owner. Dependent application of the exception waits for the applicable review; supported process capture continues. |
| Missing delivery input | Keep capacity `open` and block the calculation and commitment that depend on it. Old-case estimation may be a clearly labelled proposal only if its use is permitted; it cannot replace the missing current fact or approved fallback. Prepare the sanctioned rule and focused acquisition question independently. |
| Decision summary | Create a derived cited summary from the five facts and applicable rule, preserving source revisions, conflicts and the question's scope. Check citation coverage and meaning before presenting it. The manager receives it before deciding; the summary prepares evidence and does not decide or transfer authority. |
| Conflicting revisions | Route both relevant sources and record their identities, scopes and conflict. Keep the threshold and dependent use `open`; obtain a precedence decision from the responsible authority. A summary that silently chooses one fails Augment. |
| Whole-repository prompt | Route only the request, applicable rule, two exceptions and blank, with their provenance and controls. Name the rest as excluded context. Technical readability of 4,000 files supplies neither relevance nor permission and increases review and token cost. |
| Late context | This fails point-of-use timing. Correct content arriving after the dependent action cannot support that action retroactively. Preserve it as later evidence, reconcile the commitment under the declared route and prevent recurrence through the acquisition trigger. |
| Stale price rule | The digest proves cached bytes, not current applicability. The expiry trigger requires reacquisition from the maintained source and the opening control before calculation. Independent preparation may continue; price use remains blocked. |
| Prior-step handoff | Materialize the producer's checked output as the consumer input with attempt-qualified origin and digest before the next step opens. Preserve the producer check and route; a live link alone does not bind the bytes. |
| Shared source, two recipients | Keep one maintained source revision. Derive a cited comparison for the human and route the required structured subset to the agent, each with its own intended use and check. Do not create two rule masters or force both recipients to consume identical representations. |
| Wrong scope | Reject the annual group average for this dependent use. Matching units do not repair population, granularity or time mismatch. Acquire the plant-level daily value or keep the decision `open`. |
| Independent preparation | Prepare the evidence table and decision question with their sources and checks. Keep approval and every dependent external action blocked. Missing context blocks only the contribution that requires it. |
| Recurrent cross-source context | The recurrence, cross-source complexity, measured delay and stable interfaces can justify a maintained deterministic calculation, index or knowledge service. It binds the three source identities and revisions, sanctioned rule, checks and destination for every answer. The service reduces repeated acquisition and keeps meaning independent of the acting agent; it does not become the authority for its inputs or rule. Compare its total acquisition and review burden with the prior path before retaining it. |

## Completion rubric

A reading passes only when it:

- states context relative to a concrete contribution, recipient, use and time rather than calling a repository or graph “the context”;
- reuses an authoritative source and creates only traceable derived context;
- keeps source/provider, rule owner, calculator, reviewer and decision authority distinct;
- preserves scope, revision or validity time, actual acquisition, control, destination and freshness trigger;
- treats stale, late, conflicting, unreachable or wrong-scope context as unavailable for the dependent use;
- blocks only dependent work and preserves independently permitted preparation;
- separates a complete Augment design from actual retrieval, execution, acceptance and authority.
