# Invoice entry

`hypothesis`: candidate contribution derived from [SOP rows 1–8](../../../grundlagen/invoice-source.md#supplied-procedure); use the bound [definition](../../../grundlagen/invoice-definition.md).

## Contribution to the result

Supply a correct, identifiable bill and retained original for the disposition section. The contribution includes preparation and confirmed storage; it does not grant scheduling authority or execute payment.

## Inputs and boundaries

Controlled original, case identity, applicable rules/coding, PO or pre-entry consultation, duplicate and intake evidence, and effect permissions must be available for their dependent work. [G1–G8](../../../grundlagen/invoice-definition.md#setup-gaps-and-restricted-uses) state the acquisition decisions and restrictions. Refunds, credit-card charges, known duplicates and unsupported operations do not enter as ordinary bills.

## Workstep composition

[Prepare entry](prepare-entry/CONTEXT.md) supplies checked entry evidence, unresolved questions and any independently permitted original-filing confirmation, which need not wait for an unrelated PO/coding answer. [Record and file](record-and-file/CONTEXT.md) consumes its declared handoff, produces the bill/attachment and safely completes or confirms remaining Drive work without repeating a proved filing. Its producer-owned mappings deliver the evidence to the scheduling decision or final disposition job. Storage never acquires guessed permission or a fictitious independence premise. These two jobs separate pre-entry preparation from bill effects; screen changes alone create no jobs.

## Setup completion

Each contribution has its visible case packet and check in the child contract. Missing data and partial-effect evidence cannot masquerade as this section's result. Supported entry, missing PO, duplicate and timeout cases are reviewed in the author report. Source acquisition, checker configuration and actual write permissions remain G1–G8. No section result or harness execution is demonstrated.
